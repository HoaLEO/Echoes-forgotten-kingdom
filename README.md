# Echoes of the Forgotten Kingdom

**A 3D Fantasy Exploration & Puzzle Adventure**

*Explore ancient ruins, uncover forgotten secrets, solve mysterious puzzles, and restore the power of a lost kingdom.*

Echoes of the Forgotten Kingdom is a browser-based 3D adventure game built using **Three.js, WebGL, HTML, CSS, and JavaScript**, packaged into a single self-contained HTML file.

You are an explorer entering the ruins of a mysterious ancient civilization.

Your mission is to uncover its secrets, navigate magical landscapes, solve environmental puzzles, and collect three legendary relic fragments to unlock the ancient gateway.

Explore mysterious forests, forgotten temples, ancient courtyards, and magical sanctuaries.

## How to Play

### Controls

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| W / A / S / D | Move |
| Mouse | Look around |
| Shift | Sprint |
| Space | Jump |
| E | Interact with objects |
| Left Click | Select or manipulate puzzle objects |
| I | Open inventory |
| J | Open journal |
| Esc | Pause / Open menu |

### Game Rules

**1. Explore the World**

Travel through ancient ruins, forests, temples, and mysterious landscapes. Discover hidden passages, clues, and collectible objects.

**2. Solve Ancient Puzzles**

Complete three types of environmental puzzles:

- **Rune Puzzle:** Activate ancient symbols in the correct sequence.
- **Crystal Light Puzzle:** Rotate mirrors to redirect magical light beams toward their targets.
- **Pressure Plate Puzzle:** Move stone objects onto the correct pressure plates.

**3. Collect the Three Relics**

Each major puzzle unlocks a legendary relic fragment. Collect all three to progress toward the final objective.

**4. Unlock New Areas**

<img width="1920" height="937" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7d16f249-80b1-40c3-a52b-2883cc86e792" />



Explore the environment to discover keys, activate mechanisms, open sealed doors, and access previously unreachable locations.

**5. Complete the Final Mission**

After collecting all three relic fragments, reach the ancient gateway and activate the final mechanism to complete your adventure.

## Tips for Players

- Explore carefully. Important clues may be hidden in the environment.
- Read ancient inscriptions to understand puzzle solutions.
- Use your journal to keep track of objectives.
- Pay attention to glowing objects and unusual structures.
- Not every path is accessible immediately.
- Exploration and observation are more important than speed.

## How to Run

1. Download `index.html` from this repository.
2. Open it using a modern browser supporting WebGL, such as Chrome, Edge, or Firefox.
3. Start your adventure!

Alternatively, play directly through the game's GitHub Pages website.

**No installation required. No external game assets required.**

## Technology

- Three.js — 3D rendering
- WebGL — Real-time graphics
- JavaScript — Game mechanics
- GLSL — Custom shaders
- Web Audio API — Procedural sound
- HTML & CSS — Interface
